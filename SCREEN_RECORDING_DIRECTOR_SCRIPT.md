## 1. Pre-Recording Setup Checklist

Before you hit Record:
1. **Application Running**: Ensure `npm run dev` is active and open `http://localhost:3000/`.
2. **Browser Window**: Maximize your browser window (or set to 1920×1080) at 100% zoom.
3. **Clean Desktop**: Close extraneous browser tabs, hide bookmarks bar (`Ctrl+Shift+B` on Chrome/Edge), and mute desktop notifications.
4. **Recording Framerate**: Set your recording software (Screen Beaver, OBS, QuickTime, etc.) to 60 fps or 30 fps at 1080p.
5. **Mouse Movement Style**: Move your mouse deliberately and smoothly. Avoid frantic or jittery cursor circles; hover with intent over the elements you want the audience to notice.

## 5. Editing & Alignment Pro-Tip

1. **Record the Video First**: Follow the timecodes in Section 2 with your mouse. Don't worry about being off by 1 or 2 seconds; natural mouse pauses look authentic.
2. **Generate Voiceover**: Generate the audio file from ElevenLabs using the raw block above.
3. **Drop into Video Editor**: Place your screen recording video and the ElevenLabs audio track into any editor (CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci, or iMovie).
4. **Match Cuts**: Because each visual action aligns with a distinct spoken sentence, you can easily use ripple cuts or speed ramps (0.95x - 1.05x) to synchronize the audio and video.
