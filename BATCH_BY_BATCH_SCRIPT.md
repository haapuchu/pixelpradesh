# PixelPradesh — Batch-by-Batch Screen Recording & Voiceover Guide

This guide breaks your 2-minute product demo into **6 modular batches**. You can record your screen and generate your ElevenLabs voiceover one batch at a time, making it effortless to align and edit.

---

## Batch 1: The Hook & Market Reality

- **Timecode Window**: `00:00 – 00:24` (Duration: ~24s)
- **Screen Section**: Hero Header & Manifesto (`http://localhost:3000/`)

### Visual Action Checklist (Screen Recording)
1. **00:00 – 00:06**: Start with browser window clean at `http://localhost:3000/` at the very top.
2. **00:06 – 00:12**: Hover cursor smoothly over the main headline: *"One product. Every market. Ready to launch."*
3. **00:12 – 00:18**: Slowly sweep cursor across the multiplier badges below the headline:
   - `01 Master asset` → `04 Regional markets` → `03 Formats` → `12 Ready creatives`
4. **00:18 – 00:24**: Move cursor to the right-hand card *"Campaign contact sheet"* and pause on the live preview rows (Diwali, Durga Puja, Pongal, Ningol Chakouba).

### Narration Text (Paste into ElevenLabs for Batch 1)
> India is not a single market. It is twenty-two linguistic regions, each defined by distinct cultural festivals, visual aesthetics, and local consumer sentiments.
>
> Welcome to PixelPradesh—an autonomous media localization engine powered by Cloudinary AI. With just one master product photograph, brands can instantly generate broadcast-ready campaign creatives tailored for every corner of India.

## Batch 4: Autonomous Generation & The 12-Variant Matrix

- **Timecode Window**: `01:12 – 01:48` (Duration: ~36s)
- **Screen Section**: Loading Cockpit & Campaign Creatives Matrix (`#campaign-creatives`)

### Visual Action Checklist (Screen Recording)
1. **01:12 – 01:16**: Move cursor to the saffron button **"Generate campaign"** and click it once.
2. **01:16 – 01:20**: Observe the smooth, automatic cinematic scroll glide down to `#campaign-creatives`.
3. **01:20 – 01:30**: Keep cursor centered as the 3-step **Editorial Loading Cockpit** runs:
   - `01 Geometry Analysis` (Subject bounds & anchor lock)
   - `02 Scene Localization` (4 cultural themes & Indic scripts)
   - `03 Format Outpaint` (12 creatives & safe margins)
4. **01:30 – 01:38**: The 12-variant contact sheet renders. Pause for 3 seconds to let the full grid display.
5. **01:38 – 01:42**: Hover cursor over the **West Bengal (Durga Puja)** card to show the hover state and quality score.
6. **01:42 – 01:45**: In the matrix top-right, click **"By format"** (shows 1:1, 9:16, 16:9 columns).
7. **01:45 – 01:48**: Click **"By region"**, then click back to **"Overview (4×3)"**.

### Narration Text (Paste into ElevenLabs for Batch 4)
> With a single click... the autonomous localization pipeline triggers.
>
> The engine analyzes master product geometry... synthesizes regional festival backdrops across four Indic scripts... and outpaints the compositions with platform-safe margins.
>
> And in seconds... twelve production-grade campaign creatives are ready. Every asset preserves the authentic product silhouette while completely reimagining its cultural environment. Teams can immediately evaluate the campaign through multiple perspectives—grouped by format, or organized by regional market.

## Batch 5: Studio Lightbox & Deep Inspection

- **Timecode Window**: `01:48 – 02:16` (Duration: ~28s)
- **Screen Section**: Interactive Studio Lightbox Modal

### Visual Action Checklist (Screen Recording)
1. **01:48 – 01:52**: Click directly on the vertical **9:16 Tamil Pongal** creative card to open the Studio Lightbox.
2. **01:52 – 01:56**: Inside the Lightbox top toggle, click **"Compare"**.
3. **01:56 – 02:02**: Click and drag the Before/After split slider left and right, revealing the raw isolated master vs the localized creative.
4. **02:02 – 02:06**: Click **"Channel UI"** to activate the simulated Instagram Reels interface.
5. **02:06 – 02:10**: Click **"Safe zone: ON"** to toggle the dashed margin overlay. Click the interactive heart button.
6. **02:10 – 02:14**: In the right panel, scroll down and click **"Technical details"** accordion. Hover over `e_gen_background_replace`, `b_gen_fill`, and `l_text`. Click **"Copy recipe"** (shows green checkmark).
7. **02:14 – 02:16**: Press `Escape` on your keyboard to smoothly close the Lightbox.

### Narration Text (Paste into ElevenLabs for Batch 5)
> Clicking any asset opens the interactive Studio Lightbox. The comparison slider contrasts the raw, isolated studio shot against the fully localized festive campaign creative.
>
> Channel UI simulation validates social safe zones so text never clashes with native interfaces... while Cloudinary’s chained transformation recipe remains completely transparent and reproducible.
